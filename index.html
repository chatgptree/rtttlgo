Split into parts

For editors and firmware buffers that cap the string length. Each part is a complete, playable RTTTL with its own header.

Octave 4 is middle C (a4 = 440 Hz), matching the common Arduino RTTTL libraries. Flats, dotted notes, h for B and missing headers all parse; unreadable tokens are underlined in red rather than dropped silently.
